# Remember Me Act I Enhancement Notes

Prepared against the supplied Act I project and RememberMeScript.docx.

- Added portrait sheets for Uncle James, Aunt and Grandpa.
- Added middle-aged walking sprites for Uncle James and Aunt. Extracted the existing elderly male sprite for Grandpa without altering the original family sheet.
- Added Grandpa as an optional, quiet cemetery presence.
- Preserved every flashback dialogue line and its order. Adjusted staging, facing, the door sound, the final silence and Grace's backward step.
- Replaced the intermediate cemetery teleport with Mom approaching the family before the existing box sequence.
- Shortened repeat interactions with Mom and the flowers. Gated the exploration reminder so it does not replay after the main grave has been inspected.
- Added optional interaction hints, quiet exploration footsteps and a simplified pause menu.
- Provided English system terms and optional typography settings.

The new relatives do not introduce mandatory objectives, new backstory or medical explanations. Font settings and the final facing adjustment have not been runtime-tested. Final integration is to be checked in RPG Maker MV on the user's other computer.
